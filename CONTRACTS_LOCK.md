# CERVEL v0.1 Contract Lock

Frozen for v0.1:
- CKO is the durable top-level knowledge identity.
- Canonical CKO IDs are UUIDv7.
- CKURI canonical form uses immutable CKO identity.
- Artifact storage location does not determine identity.
- Fragments are durable citation anchors.
- Embeddings are regenerable derivatives.
- Claims and relationships preserve evidence and epistemic state.
- Persistent AI transformations require provenance.
- Authorization occurs before retrieval.
- CCP is the model-neutral boundary between knowledge and intelligence.
- PostgreSQL + pgvector + S3-compatible storage is the initial production architecture.

Deferred:
- federation wire protocol
- ontology DSL
- CRDT editing
- dedicated graph database
- mandatory cryptographic signatures
- public standards governance
